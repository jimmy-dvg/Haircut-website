FAQ

What to expect before, during, and after your ritual.

How should I arrive?

Wear your everyday texture—air-dried or styled the way you typically leave the house. It lets us read movement accurately.

Do you work with curls?

Yes. We map curls dry, then hydrate and refine with slide cutting to avoid shrink surprises.

Can I bundle services?

Absolutely. Most guests stack a cut with either Chromatic Studio or Care Rituals for a seamless finish.

What is the booking window?

We release appointments six weeks out. Text or email us to join the standby list if you need something sooner.
